Privacy Policy – Rubble

Last updated: September 25, 2026

This privacy policy explains how the mobile game Rubble ("the game", "we", "us") handles information. Rubble is developed by an independent developer, TCM Dev. If you have any questions, contact us at tcm.developing@gmail.com.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves a small amount of data only on your own device:

  • the player name you type in (optional; the default is "PILOT"),
  • your best scores (a local leaderboard),
  • your game settings (sound, music, vibration).

This data is never sent to us. We do not run any servers, and we do not ask you to create an account. Uninstalling the game deletes this data.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game is free and shows optional rewarded video ads (for example, to continue a run). Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC / Google Ireland Limited. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, the AdMob software in the game may automatically collect:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID),
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (device model, operating system, app version, language),
  • interactions with ads (for example, whether an ad was viewed or tapped),
  • diagnostic information (for example, crash and performance data of the ad software).

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. You can learn more here: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Your consent (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, using Google's consent tool. You can choose to accept, reject, or manage your choices. If you do not consent, you may still see non-personalised (limited) ads. You can change your choice at any time from the game's settings menu (Privacy settings).

You can also reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

3b. Your privacy choices (United States)

If you live in a U.S. state with a consumer privacy law (for example California, Virginia, Colorado, Connecticut or Utah), you have the right to opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your personal information for targeted advertising. You can do this at any time from the game's settings menu (Privacy settings). After you opt out, Google will only use your data for limited purposes (restricted data processing) and you may still see non-personalised ads. We do not sell your personal information for money.

4. Children

Rubble is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through the game, please contact us and we will help remove it.

5. Your rights

Under data-protection laws such as the GDPR, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your personal data. Because we do not store your data ourselves, most requests concerning advertising data should be directed to Google. We are happy to help: just email us.

6. Security

Data sent by the ad software is encrypted in transit. Data saved on your device stays on your device.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game changes (for example, if we add new features). The latest version will always be available on this page, with the date at the top.
