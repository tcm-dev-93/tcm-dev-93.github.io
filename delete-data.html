Delete your data – Rubble: Moon Rover Defense

Developer: TCM Dev · Last updated: October 3, 2026

This page explains how to delete the data that the game Rubble: Moon Rover Defense (Google Play, com.tcm.rubble) stores about you.

Option 1 – Delete it yourself in the game (instant)

  1. Open Rubble.
  2. On the main menu, tap your pilot name to open the PILOT page.
  3. Tap "Delete my world-board data", then tap it again to confirm.

This immediately deletes your anonymous online player account on Unity Gaming Services, your pilot name and all your scores on the world leaderboard.

Option 2 – Ask us by email

Send an email to tcm.developing@gmail.com with the subject "Rubble data deletion" and the pilot name you use in the game (and, if you can, a screenshot of your row on the world leaderboard). We will delete your world-leaderboard data within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted

  • Your anonymous Unity player ID
  • Your pilot name on the world leaderboard
  • Your scores and run details (rover, rover level, bosses, revives, run time, meteors)

What is not stored by us / other data

  • Data on your phone (progress, Moon Dust, rovers, settings, local scores) is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its app data in Android settings.
  • Google Play Games (optional sign-in): your Play Games profile and its scores are managed by Google. You can delete them in the Google Play Games app or in your Google Account (myaccount.google.com).
  • Advertising data (Google AdMob) is processed by Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • We do not keep backups of world-leaderboard data after it is deleted.
